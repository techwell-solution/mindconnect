Self Assessments

Take evidence-based mental health assessments to better understand your emotional wellbeing. Your responses remain private and can help guide discussions with your counsellor.

Before You Begin

These self-assessments are confidential screening tools designed to help you better understand your mental wellbeing. They are not a substitute for a professional diagnosis, but they can help you and your counsellor identify areas that may need attention.
